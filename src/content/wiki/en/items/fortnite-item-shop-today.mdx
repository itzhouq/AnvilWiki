## What time does the Fortnite item shop reset today?

The shop resets every day at 0:00 UTC. In November the United States is on standard time, so that is **7 PM Eastern, 4 PM Pacific, and 8 AM the next morning in Japan**; during daylight saving time the Eastern slot moves to 8 PM. Epic's own help documentation confirms the midnight-UTC schedule and suggests checking the "About Shop" panel in-game if you need the exact minute on your platform. A practical habit: check the shop right after reset if you are hunting a specific rotation, since popular items can disappear within a single daily slot.

## How does the daily shop rotation actually work?

Each daily reset swaps a curated set of sections rather than the whole catalog at once. Featured items usually anchor the top of the shop for one to several days, while smaller sections — individual outfit bundles, emotes, gear — rotate faster. Most items eventually return after they leave, though event exclusives tied to a specific collaboration may not. Prices stay fixed per item: the shop never discounts V-Bucks prices during the season, which is exactly why the [Pirate Shop Access token](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/) matters — it is the only mechanic this season that makes the entire V-Bucks catalog free, and it does it for 14 days rather than a single reset.

## Does the shop ever give free items?

Yes, but not through the shop itself. Free rewards this season flow through the Treasure Hunters chest system: opening chests on Crossbones Isle can drop the current [weekly gold outfit](/items/fortnite-gold-outfits/), loading screens, jam tracks, and the ladder of smaller weekly tiers. If a genuine redeem code ever goes live, it is redeemed on Epic's own page — our [codes guide](/codes/fortnite-codes/) tracks the current status and the scams to avoid. Anything inside the shop itself, outside a token window, costs V-Bucks.

## When should you check the shop this season?

Three moments beat random daily browsing. Saturday evenings align with Treasure Hours (2–4 PM and 9–11 PM ET), when boosted chest odds make the token — and therefore a free shop — a live possibility. Sunday is the weekly gold outfit reveal, the season's most-watched moment. And the captain arrival dates above put fresh collaboration content in the shop on predictable days.
