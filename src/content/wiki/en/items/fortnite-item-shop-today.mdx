The item shop is the one page on this site that changes every single day, so this guide covers what a static page can honestly provide: the exact reset time, this week's dated captain arrivals, how the rotation behaves, and where to check the live list the moment you want it.

## When does the item shop reset?

The shop resets every day at **0:00 UTC**. In November the United States is on standard time, so that is **7 PM Eastern, 4 PM Pacific, and 8 AM the next morning in Japan**; during daylight saving time the Eastern slot moves to 8 PM. Epic's own help documentation confirms the midnight-UTC schedule and suggests checking the "About Shop" panel in-game if you need the exact minute on your platform. A practical habit: check right after reset if you are hunting a specific rotation, since popular items can disappear within a single daily slot.

## What is in the item shop today

Each daily reset swaps a curated set of sections rather than the whole catalog at once. Featured items usually anchor the top of the shop for one to several days, while smaller sections — individual outfit bundles, emotes, gear — rotate faster. Most items eventually return after they leave, though event exclusives tied to a specific collaboration may not. Prices stay fixed per item: the shop never discounts V-Bucks prices during the season, which is exactly why the [Pirate Shop Access token](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/) matters — it is the only mechanic this season that makes the entire V-Bucks catalog free, and it does it for 14 days rather than a single reset.

The shop itself does not hand out free items. Free rewards this season flow through the Treasure Hunters chest system: opening chests on Crossbones Isle can drop the current [weekly gold outfit](/items/fortnite-gold-outfits/), loading screens, jam tracks, and the ladder of smaller weekly tiers. If a genuine redeem code ever goes live, it is redeemed on Epic's own page — our [codes guide](/codes/fortnite-codes/) tracks the current status and the scams to avoid.

## How to check the live rotation

For the rotation as it exists right now, the official [Fortnite item shop page](https://www.fortnite.com/item-shop) is the only real-time source — this page tracks the schedule and mechanics rather than duplicating a daily list that would be stale within hours. Two habits make the check cheap: look right after the 0:00 UTC reset for the fullest view of a new rotation, and use the in-game "About Shop" panel to confirm the reset minute on your platform. Saturday evenings align with Treasure Hours (2–4 PM and 9–11 PM ET), when the [boosted chest odds](/guides/pirate-token-odds/) make a free-shop token a live possibility, and Sundays bring the weekly [gold outfit reveal](/guides/fortnite-event-schedule/).
