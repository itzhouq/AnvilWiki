## What is the weekly gold outfit in Fortnite?

The Fortnite weekly gold outfit is a free, gold-styled celebrity skin that Epic Games gives away through the Treasure Hunters chest event — it never appears in the item shop. A new Solid Gold outfit is revealed every Sunday during the season, and the current week's skin can only drop from chests you open in matches. It is the rarest reward in the entire chest pool, which is why the Sunday reveal has become the most-watched moment of the week.

![Crossbones Isle, where Treasure Hunters chests spawn — image: Epic Games, Fortnite Treasure Hunters Week 2 trailer](/images/articles/treasure-hunters-crossbones-isle.jpg)

## Which gold outfit is live this week?

**Solid Gold Lil Uzi Vert** is live. Epic revealed it on Sunday, November 8, 2026, and it stays the weekly drop until the next Sunday reveal replaces it. Each week's skin ties to that week's captain — Lil Uzi Vert's week follows A$AP Rocky's — and five reveals are planned before the season ends on December 5. The full lineup is in the release schedule section below.

## Weekly gold outfit drop rate and odds

Every rate below is Epic Games' officially confirmed number, published in November 2026 — not a community estimate. The skin sits at the very top of the chest reward ladder:

| Reward | Base odds | Treasure Hours | Boosted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Gold Outfit | 1 in 1,562,500 | 7.5x | ≈ 1 in 208,333 |
| Pirate Shop Access (token) | 1 in 137,500 | 1.5x | ≈ 1 in 91,667 |

### Base odds: 1 in 1,562,500

At the base rate, one chest in 1,562,500 produces the current gold outfit — roughly 0.000064% per chest. That makes the weekly skin more than eleven times rarer than the Pirate Shop Access token, and the rate is identical for every player, with no new-account or returning-player bonus.

### Treasure Hour odds: 1 in 208,333

During the Saturday Treasure Hours, the per-chest rate improves about 7.5-fold to roughly 1 in 208,333. Those two windows — 2–4 PM and 9–11 PM ET — are the only time the odds move; the complete reward ladder and multiplier rules live on the [Pirate Token odds page](/guides/pirate-token-odds/).

### How the 7.5x boost works

The multiplier applies per chest, not per week: a Treasure Hour chest is seven and a half times likelier to roll the rare tier than the same chest opened on a Tuesday. It does not accumulate — chest 1 and chest 500 carry the same boosted rate, and nothing pities you toward a guarantee.

<Callout type="warn" title="One gold outfit per week">
You can only earn each treasure type once per week (Gizmos are the exception). After this week's gold outfit drops, further chests roll the remaining rewards — so early-week finds are worth the most.
</Callout>

## How to get the weekly gold outfit

There is exactly one method: open Treasure Hunters chests on Crossbones Isle and let the odds resolve. A practical grind looks like this:

1. Load the Treasure Hunters mode — the 80-player Crossbones Isle map. Standard battle royale chests do not count.
2. Land at a high-density POI (Rubber Resort and Lantern Landing hold the most chests) and sweep spawns methodically rather than fighting.
3. Prioritize Saturdays: the two Treasure Hours multiply your effective odds by 7.5x for this tier.
4. Use the Lucky Locator Gizmo when the wheel offers it — spawned Sprite Chests add free rolls.

The honest framing after week one: most players will not see the skin drop naturally this season. Treat it as a lottery ticket attached to a normal match, and let the guaranteed smaller tiers — loading screens, jam tracks, pickaxes — be the reason you open chests at all.

## Can you buy the gold outfit in the item shop?

No. Epic has not offered any Solid Gold outfit for sale, and the official Treasure Hunters announcement describes them exclusively as chest finds. Watch for impersonators here too: any shop listing, code, or third-party offer claiming to sell you a Solid Gold skin is a scam — the same warning applies to [fake redeem codes](/codes/fortnite-codes/). The [Pirate Shop token](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/) does not unlock gold outfits either, since it only discounts the regular V-Bucks catalog.
