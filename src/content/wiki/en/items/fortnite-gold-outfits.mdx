## What is the weekly gold outfit in Fortnite?

The weekly gold outfit is a free, gold-styled celebrity skin that Epic Games gives away through the Treasure Hunters chest event — it never appears in the item shop. A new Solid Gold outfit is revealed every Sunday during the season, and the current week's skin can only drop from chests you open in matches. It is the rarest reward in the entire chest pool, which is why the weekly reveal has become the most-watched moment of the season.

Each week's skin ties to that week's captain — the in-game celebrity whose battleship POI anchors the map. Week 1 belonged to A$AP Rocky, week 2 to Lil Uzi Vert, and the Sunday reveals are expected to continue matching the weekly captain lineup through the end of the season on December 5.

## What are the odds of getting the weekly gold outfit?

Epic Games officially confirmed the odds: **1 in 1,562,500 chests** at base rate, which works out to roughly 0.000064% per chest. That makes the gold outfit more than eleven times rarer than the Pirate Shop Access token (1 in 137,500). During Saturday Treasure Hours, the gold outfit's odds get a 7.5x boost, bringing it to about **1 in 208,333** — still vanishingly rare, but the best window you will ever get.

<Callout type="warn" title="One gold outfit per week">
You can only earn each treasure type once per week (Gizmos are the exception). Opening more chests after the weekly gold outfit drops switches your loot to the remaining rewards, so early-week finds are worth the most.
</Callout>

## How do you get the gold outfit?

There is exactly one method: open Treasure Hunters chests on Crossbones Isle and let the odds resolve. A practical grind looks like this:

1. Load the Treasure Hunters mode (the 80-player Crossbones Isle map) — chests only drop there, not in standard battle royale.
2. Land at a high-density POI and sweep chest spawns methodically rather than fighting.
3. Prioritize Saturdays: the two Treasure Hours windows multiply your effective odds by 7.5x for the gold outfit tier.
4. Use the Lucky Locator Gizmo when it appears — spawned Sprite Chests add extra rolls at no cost.

The community consensus after week one is blunt: most players will not see a gold outfit drop naturally this season. Treat it as a lottery ticket attached to a normal match, and let the guaranteed smaller tiers — loading screens, jam tracks, pickaxes — be the reason you open chests at all.

## Can you buy the gold outfit in the item shop?

No. Epic has not offered any Solid Gold outfit for sale, and the official Treasure Hunters announcement describes them exclusively as chest finds. Watch for impersonators here too: any shop listing, code, or third-party offer claiming to sell you the weekly gold outfit is a scam — the same warning applies to [fake redeem codes](/codes/fortnite-codes/). The only legitimate path is opening chests during the week the skin is live, and the [Pirate Shop token](/items/) does not unlock gold outfits either, since it only discounts the regular V-Bucks catalog.
