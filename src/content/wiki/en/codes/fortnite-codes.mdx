## How to redeem a code

Redemption happens on Epic's official page, not inside the game client. The flow takes about a minute:

1. Open [fortnite.com/redeemcode](https://www.fortnite.com/redeemcode) in a browser and log in with your Epic Games account.
2. Type or paste the code exactly as printed — most promo codes are short strings of letters and numbers.
3. Confirm the reward shown on the redemption screen, then check your locker in-game after the next shop refresh.

Two things to know before you paste anything. First, some codes are tied to a specific platform or region, and Epic's page will reject them on other accounts — that rejection is normal, not a sign the code is fake. Second, V-Bucks gift cards are a different system: those are redeemed through the same page but print a different code format, and they load V-Bucks rather than a cosmetic item.

## Scam codes to avoid

<Callout type="danger" title="No generator works — ever">
Sites offering a "Fortnite code generator" or unlimited free V-Bucks codes are scams. There is no way to generate valid codes outside Epic's own systems, and these sites exist to harvest account logins or push ads.
</Callout>

The red flags repeat across every scam variant this season:

- **Login requests.** Epic never needs your password to hand you a reward. Any site that asks you to "sign in to claim a code" is phishing your account.
- **Human verification walls.** "Complete one offer to unlock your code" is an ad-fraud funnel, not a reward.
- **Urgency pressure.** Real codes stay redeemable for days or weeks; scammers invent five-minute countdowns.
- **Token sellers.** During the Treasure Hunters season, some players charge $200–250 to "sell" a Pirate Shop Access token. The token cannot be traded or gifted, so every one of these offers is either a scam or an account-sharing risk that can get an account banned.

Legitimate sources for a real code are Epic's own channels only: the official Fortnite website and social accounts, in-game news, and e-mail receipts from Epic for purchases you actually made.
