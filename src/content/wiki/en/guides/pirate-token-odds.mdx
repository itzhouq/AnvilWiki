## Pirate Shop Access token: 1 in 137,500

The Pirate Shop Access token — the "Pirate Token" — carries an officially confirmed drop rate of **1 in 137,500 Treasure Hunters chests**, roughly 0.00073% per chest. Epic Games published the number in November 2026 after a week of community guessing, and attached two conditions: the rate is identical for every player, with no new-account, returning-player, or platform bonus, and Treasure Hours on Saturdays multiply it by 1.5x, to roughly 1 in 91,667.

Working from the official rate, the practical expectancy looks like this:

| Chests opened | Approx. chance of at least one token |
| --- | --- |
| 360 (a thorough full-map clear) | ~0.26% |
| 10,000 | ~7% |
| 50,000 | ~31% |
| 137,500 | ~63% |

Even the last row is the uncomfortable one: opening 137,500 chests — about 380 flawless full-map clears — still leaves a one-in-three chance of never seeing a token. Drop odds do not pity-timer; chest 1 and chest 137,500 carry the same rate, and opening more chests only adds independent attempts. What a find is worth is covered on the [Pirate Shop page](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/), and the chest-opening method itself lives on the [step-by-step guide](/guides/how-to-get-pirate-token/).

<Callout type="warn" title="The 1-in-700,000 figure is a myth">
A community estimate pegging the token at "1 in 700,000" spread before Epic published real numbers — the official rate is five times better. Week-one "token sellers" charging $200–250 were scams for the same reason: the token is bound to the account that finds it and cannot be traded.
</Callout>

## Weekly gold outfit: 1 in 1,562,500

The weekly gold outfit is the rarest drop in the pool: **1 in 1,562,500 chests** at base rate — about 0.000064%, more than eleven times rarer than the token. During Saturday Treasure Hours a 7.5x multiplier improves it to roughly 1 in 208,333, still the hardest roll in the game. The skin rotates every Sunday and is never sold in the shop; the [weekly gold outfit tracker](/items/fortnite-gold-outfits/) follows the rotation and dates.

## Treasure Hour boosts and multipliers

Treasure Hours are the only time the odds move. Boosts are tier-specific:

| Reward | Boost | Boosted odds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Gold Outfit | 7.5x | ≈ 1 in 208,333 |
| Pirate Shop Access | 1.5x (one confirmed event ran 2x) | ≈ 1 in 91,667 |
| Weekly Vehicle & Decal | 10x | ≈ 1 in 781 |

The multiplier can vary by the theme of the individual Treasure Hour — Epic confirmed one 2x token window in the season's first week. The windows run twice every Saturday, 2–4 PM and 9–11 PM ET, through the season finale; the full dated calendar is on the [event schedule](/guides/fortnite-event-schedule/).

## The full chest reward ladder

This is the complete Treasure Hunters chest ladder at Epic's officially published rates — the only page on this site that carries the whole table:

| Reward | Base odds (per chest) | Treasure Hours boost | Boosted odds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Gold Outfit | 1 in 1,562,500 | 7.5x | ≈ 1 in 208,333 |
| Pirate Shop Access (token) | 1 in 137,500 | 1.5x | ≈ 1 in 91,667 |
| Weekly Vehicle & Decal | 1 in 7,813 | 10x | ≈ 1 in 781 |
| Weekly Pickaxe or Back Bling | 1 in 568 | — | — |
| Weekly Jam Track | 1 in 391 | — | — |
| Weekly Loading Screen | 1 in 272 | — | — |
| Any Gizmo | 1 in 127 | — | — |

Two reading notes: each treasure type can only drop once per week (Gizmos are unlimited), so the ladder hands you the small tiers quickly and saves the rare ones; and only the top three rows receive a Treasure Hours multiplier — the daily tiers keep their base rates all week.

### Vehicle and decal tier: 1 in 7,813

The vehicle skin and decal tier gets the largest Treasure Hours boost — 10x to roughly 1 in 781 — which makes Saturday the realistic window for it.

### Gizmo tier: 1 in 127

Any Gizmo drops from one chest in 127, the most common roll above the daily tiers. Gizmos are the one treasure type without a weekly cap.
