Epic launched the Treasure Hunters mini-season on November 1, 2026 and pegged its finale to the Chapter 7 end event on December 5 — both dates come from Epic's official [Treasure Hunters announcement](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-treasure-hunters-the-greatest-adventure-of-all-time), which is also where future schedule changes will land first. This page lines up every dated moment announced so far — Treasure Hours, Mastery Mondays, Captain's Days, the gold outfit reveals, and the finale — with the times converted for your timezone.

## What Treasure Hours are and when they run

Treasure Hours are the season's boosted-odds windows, and they run **every Saturday in two repeating slots: 2–4 PM ET and 9–11 PM ET** — the late window is the one players shorthand as "9–11 PM." Each event carries a theme that can nudge the multipliers; the season's first was "Treasure Hours: Fortune." Chest odds move only during these windows — the exact boosted numbers live on the [Pirate Token odds page](/guides/pirate-token-odds/).

## Saturday Treasure Hour times (ET)

The windows are announced in US Eastern time, which sits at UTC-5 in November:

| Local zone | 2–4 PM ET window | 9–11 PM ET window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UTC | 19:00–21:00 Saturday | 02:00–04:00 Sunday |
| UK (GMT) | 19:00–21:00 Saturday | 02:00–04:00 Sunday |
| Central Europe (CET) | 20:00–22:00 Saturday | 03:00–05:00 Sunday |
| Japan (JST) | 04:00–06:00 Sunday | 11:00–13:00 Sunday |
| US Pacific (PST) | 11:00–13:00 Saturday | 18:00–20:00 Saturday |

The late window is brutal for Europe and Japan — that is exactly why the earlier 2 PM ET session exists, and why Saturday afternoon Pacific time is the friendliest slot on the calendar.

## Mastery Mondays

Every Monday during the season, matches start with captain weapons available from the drop — no loot hunt required. It is the midweek complement to the Saturday boosts: nothing about the chest odds changes, but the matches themselves run hotter, which makes Monday a good day to clear weekly treasure caps before the Saturday grind.

## Captain's Days

Five dates are announced: **November 1, 8, 12, 15, and 22**. Each one adds new captain weapons and Sprites to the island alongside the week's active captain. Shop arrivals ride the same rhythm — A$AP Rocky landed November 5, Zoro, Sanji and the Chopper Sidekick on November 7, Lil Uzi Vert's cosmetics on November 12, Buggy on November 13, and Cap'n Hatsune Miku on November 14. The running list is on the [item shop page](/items/fortnite-item-shop-today/).

## Gold outfit reveal dates

Weekly Solid Gold reveals land on Sundays: November 1 brought Solid Gold Rugahand (A$AP Rocky), November 8 revealed Solid Gold Lil Uzi Vert — the current skin — and three more are expected on **November 15, 22, and 29** before the finale. The rotation, odds and drop method are tracked on the [weekly gold outfit page](/items/fortnite-gold-outfits/).

## Season finale: December 5, 2026

The season closes with the **Chapter 7 end event on Saturday, December 5** — Epic is teasing it as an unforgettable send-off. One date matters more than any of them for token hunters: the **first Pirate Shop Access token's validity window closes December 15**, ten days after the season ends, so a find in the final weeks still has room to be spent. The second token waits in reserve for 2027. If you are still short on the math, start with [how to hunt the token](/guides/how-to-get-pirate-token/) and keep the [Pirate Shop rules](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/) handy for the moment one drops.
