## When is the next Treasure Hour in Fortnite?

Treasure Hours run **every Saturday** for the rest of the Treasure Hunters season, in two repeating windows: **2–4 PM ET** and **9–11 PM ET** — the late window is the one players shorthand as "9–11 PM." Both windows carry the boosted chest rates (1.5x for the Pirate Shop Access token, 7.5x for the weekly gold outfit, 10x for the vehicle tier), and each event has its own theme that can nudge the multipliers — the season's first was "Treasure Hours: Fortune." If you are reading this on a Saturday, the next window is simply the earlier of the two you have not missed.

## What actually happens during a Treasure Hour?

Three things at once. Chest odds jump on the tiered multipliers — the [full odds table](/guides/pirate-token-odds/) breaks down every rate. Sprites get more active: the Fortune-themed hour boosted Fortune Sprite spawns, and Sprites feed the Sprite Dust economy that powers your Treasure tab progression. And matches start with a special loadout — the November 7 event dropped everyone in with A$AP Rocky's Shock Rocket plus a Self-Revive Device, turning the grind sessions into genuinely different matches.

## What time is 9 PM ET in my timezone?

The Treasure Hours are announced in US Eastern time, which sits at UTC-5 in November:

| Local zone | 2–4 PM ET window | 9–11 PM ET window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UTC | 19:00–21:00 Saturday | 02:00–04:00 Sunday |
| UK (GMT) | 19:00–21:00 Saturday | 02:00–04:00 Sunday |
| Central Europe (CET) | 20:00–22:00 Saturday | 03:00–05:00 Sunday |
| Japan (JST) | 04:00–06:00 Sunday | 11:00–13:00 Sunday |
| US Pacific (PST) | 11:00–13:00 Saturday | 18:00–20:00 Saturday |

The late window is brutal for Europe and Japan — that is exactly why the earlier 2 PM ET session exists, and why Saturday afternoon Pacific time is the friendliest slot on the calendar.

## What is still ahead before the season ends?

Three Sundays of gold outfit reveals (November 15, 22, 29), at least two more Captain's Days, weekly Treasure Hours through the end of the month — and the December 5 finale, which Epic is teasing as an unforgettable send-off for Chapter 7. One date matters more than any of them for token hunters: the **first token's validity window closes December 15**, ten days after the season ends, so a find in the final weeks still has room to be spent. The second token waits in reserve for 2027. If you are still short on the math, start with [how to hunt the token](/guides/how-to-get-pirate-token/) and keep the [Pirate Shop rules](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/) handy for the moment one drops.
