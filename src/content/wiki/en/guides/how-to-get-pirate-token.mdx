The Pirate Token — officially "Pirate Shop Access" — is an ultra-rare chest drop that makes every V-Bucks item in the shop free for 14 days when activated. The official rate is **1 in 137,500 chests**, identical for every player; the complete number set lives on the [Pirate Token odds page](/guides/pirate-token-odds/). It cannot be bought, traded, or earned through quests, so the hunting method — maximizing chest openings per hour — is the entire game. Here is the route, step by step.

## Step 1: Load the Treasure Hunters mode

Token hunting only works in the right playlist. Launch the Treasure Hunters mode — the 80-player Crossbones Isle map — because chests outside it do not roll the event loot table at all. Standard battle royale chests, including the ones on the old island, have never produced a token.

If you are queueing with a squad, agree on the plan before dropping: the chest grind is a movement discipline, not a combat one, and mixed-intent squads are why the community spent week one arguing about "ruined" matches.

## Step 2: Run a high-density chest route on Crossbones Isle

The map holds roughly 360 Treasure Hunt chests across 901 spawn points, 243 of which are guaranteed spawns. Community surveys of the island agree on the density ranking:

| POI | Approx. chest count |
| --- | --- |
| Rubber Resort | 47 |
| Lantern Landing | 45 |
| Loguetown | 41 |
| Arlong Park | 39 |
| Loot Lagoon | 26 |
| Lil Uzi's Stronghold | ~20+ |

Rubber Resort and Lantern Landing are the standard drops: both sit near the middle of the island, and Arlong Park is close enough to Rubber Resort to chain into the same circuit. Loguetown rewards players comfortable with vertical sweeps, since its chests climb the buildings. Set a loop, not a sprint — sweep every spawn point in a tight rotation rather than chasing loot across the map, and ignore fights: every minute in combat is a minute not opening chests.

## Step 3: Use the Lucky Locator Gizmo

Gizmos are the consumable Sprite tools on the Gizmo Wheel (hold your pickaxe button to open it), and one of them is a direct boost to this grind: the **Lucky Locator** spawns a buried Sprite Chest with guaranteed loot, adding free rolls to your count at no cost. Fire the firework Gizmo while you move between circuits too — the falling Sprite Dust bottles feed your Treasure tab progress while your route continues.

Roll every Gizmo the wheel offers during a chest run. They cost nothing but the button press, and each spawned chest is one more draw against the 1-in-137,500 rate.

## Step 4: Grind Saturday Treasure Hours

Timing is the only lever that changes the odds themselves. **Treasure Hours run twice every Saturday — 2–4 PM and 9–11 PM ET — with a 1.5x boost to the token rate**, roughly 1 in 91,667. Individual events can run hotter; Epic confirmed one 2x window in the season's first week. The rest of the weekly rhythm helps too: Mastery Mondays add captain weapons at match start, and Captain's Days (November 1, 8, 12, 15, and 22) flood the island with new Sprites and weapons. Every remaining dated window is listed on the [event schedule](/guides/fortnite-event-schedule/).

<Callout type="tip" title="Weekly caps reset, not progress">
Each treasure type can drop once per week (Gizmos unlimited). If you already claimed this week's loading screen or jam track, further chests keep rolling the rare tiers — so late-week grinding is pure token odds.
</Callout>

## What the Pirate Token unlocks

The token lands silently in your Treasure tab the moment the chest opens, and you get two: one usable November 1 to December 15, 2026, and one valid through all of 2027. Nothing auto-activates — you choose the moment, and since each activation starts a fixed 14-day clock of free V-Bucks shop items, the smart play is waiting for a shop rotation you actually want. The full rules, including what stays excluded, are on the [Pirate Shop page](/items/fortnite-pirate-shop/).

## Scam warnings: no token sellers, no generators

<Callout type="danger" title="Nobody can sell you a token">
The token is bound to the account that finds it. Every "token for sale" offer — week-one sellers asked $200–250 — is either a scam or a setup for account theft. Epic cannot transfer tokens between accounts, and there is no code that grants one.
</Callout>

Three rules cover it. Never share your account credentials, no matter what the seller promises to "open" on your behalf. Never trust a site with a token generator or a shop-access exploit — the drop is server-side, so no client trick can produce it. And treat any [redeem code](/codes/fortnite-codes/) claiming to grant shop access as fake by definition, since codes and chest drops are separate systems.
